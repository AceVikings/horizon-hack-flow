The old way

Give the agent a hot wallet and hope.

  • One bad prompt can spend everything. Nothing sits between the model and the wallet.
  • A stolen key can withdraw it all. The key that trades is the key that owns the money.
  • Any price goes through. A token allowance doesn't know what a fair price is.
  • No record of why. After a bad night you have balances, not reasons.

The Flow way

Put the money in a vault that checks every trade.

  • Hard caps on-chain. $100 a trade, $250 a day and $150 held, per vault. The agent can't raise them.
  • The agent key can't withdraw. It can trade inside the limits or pause. Only your owner key moves funds out.
  • Bad prices revert. Each trade is checked against the Supra oracle price, with one pool and one router fixed at deploy.
  • Every decision is written down. Trades and skipped trades go in a journal; each trade's reason hash goes on-chain.

How it works

Three steps. Then let it run.

  1. 01

    Set the limits

    Flow deploys one vault per market (WHSK/USDT and WBTC/USDT) and you fund it. You choose the per-trade, per-day and exposure caps. The owner and guardian keys stay on your own devices, never on the agent's machine.

  2. 02

    Let the agent trade

    Flow watches the HSKSwap pools, the Supra price and the HashKey Global order books, and lines up candidate trades. The agent picks one, sizes it down or skips. The vault checks the trade on-chain and swaps on HSKSwap. The output stays in the vault.

  3. 03

    Check the record

    Every decision lands in the journal with its reason. Trades carry the reason hash on-chain and an EAS attestation. Stop-loss and take-profit run without asking the model, and you can pause any time.
